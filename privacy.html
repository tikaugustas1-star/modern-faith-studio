Privacy Policy

Information we use

When you connect TikTok, Modern Faith Studio may receive basic account information and authorization tokens required for the features you approve. We use this information to identify your connected account and perform authorized publishing actions.

What we do not collect

We do not request or store your TikTok password. We do not sell personal information or use connected account data for unrelated advertising.

Storage and security

Authorization data should be stored securely and used only for the approved integration. You may revoke access from your TikTok account settings.

Third-party services

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Modern Faith Studio is not responsible for changes to third-party services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Modern Faith Studio account owner through the associated project account.
